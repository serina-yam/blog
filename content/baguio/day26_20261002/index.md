There was a power outage while I was having breakfast.

It was already the second one since I came here.  
This is Philippine life, I guess 😂

I also saw a huge stack of bags of rice on the first floor.  
Apparently, the school is very well prepared.

![Bags of rice stacked on the first floor](IMG_5874.JPG)

I couldn't get a massage because I hadn't made a reservation, so I got some takoyaki on the way back instead.

Lesson learned: next time, make a reservation!!!

The takoyaki tasted pretty similar to what I'm used to in Japan, except for the sauce and mayo.

![Takoyaki being cooked](IMG_5895.JPG)

![Takoyaki with sauce and toppings](IMG_5896.JPG)

I also tried cucumber juice.  
Honestly, I didn't expect much because cucumber juice didn't sound very tasty, but it was surprisingly good!

![Cucumber juice](IMG_5902.JPG)

![A sleeping cat at the cafe](IMG_5904.JPG)

It was my first time using a Korean-style photo booth.

I finally got to experience the Korean-style purikura everyone seems to love!

![Korean-style photo booth](IMG_5913.JPG)

![Korean-style photo strip](IMG_6104.JPG)

I spotted these cookies and immediately added them to my souvenir shopping list.

The tins are soooo cute.
Honestly, the packaging alone almost convinced me to buy them.

![Colorful souvenir cookie tins](IMG_5940.JPG)

![Manila cookie tins](IMG_5941.JPG)

![Back of a souvenir cookie tin](IMG_5942.JPG)

![Cebu cookie tin](IMG_5943.JPG)

I haven't bought the tins yet, but I did buy some of the cookies first to see if they actually taste good 😂

![Souvenirs and snacks I bought](IMG_5949.jpg)

![A small toy from a snack](IMG_5958.jpg)

![Soaps I bought as souvenirs](IMG_5968.jpg)

We had a late-night meal because my friends weren't leaving until around 11 p.m.

Eating together this late made me think,
“Yep, I'm definitely living the student life right now.” 😂

![Our late-night meal](IMG_5959.JPG)
